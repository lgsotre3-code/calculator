Mortgage Calculator — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

MortgageCalc ("we", "us", "our") operates www.mortgage-pro-calc.com (the "Website"), a free mortgage calculator that also provides amortization schedules and payment breakdowns in multiple languages.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the calculator. The numbers you enter are processed in your browser and are not stored on our servers. We may collect limited, non-personal information automatically, such as browser type, device type, language preference, and pages visited.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local storage in your browser to remember your preferred language so you do not have to choose it on every visit. This data never leaves your device.

We also use third-party services described below that may set cookies:

  • Google AdSense — to display ads. Google uses cookies (including DoubleClick cookies) to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Data is anonymized when possible.

3. Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.google.com/settings/ads.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect information about how the Website is used (pages visited, time on page, approximate location, browser). This data helps us improve content and user experience. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the Website, display relevant advertising, understand usage trends, and maintain security.

6. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites (for example, lender pre-approval partners). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the Website (analytics and advertising) and where required by law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to opt out of data collection. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@mortgage-pro-calc.com.

9. Children's Privacy

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@mortgage-pro-calc.com.
