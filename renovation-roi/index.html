Do Home Renovations Pay for Themselves?

Most renovations recover only part of their cost at resale. A kitchen remodel typically recovers 60%â€“80% of its cost, while a bathroom remodel recovers a similar share. The value increase also depends on your home's current value, your neighborhood and how the project compares with the rest of the market. This calculator applies your expected appreciation percentage to your current value and shows the resulting profit or loss.

How to Calculate Renovation ROI

To find the ROI, subtract the renovation cost from the value increase it creates, then divide by the cost and multiply by 100. For example, a $25,000 renovation that raises the home's value by $30,000 produces a $5,000 profit and a 20% ROI. If the increase is smaller than the cost, the project loses money at resale â€” even if it improves the home for your own enjoyment.

Kitchen and bathroom remodels consistently recover the highest share of cost at resale, followed by paint, flooring and exterior siding. Keeping finishes neutral and in line with the neighborhood maximizes recovery.
